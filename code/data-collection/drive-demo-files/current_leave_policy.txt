BELGIUM PAID LEAVE POLICY — 2026

Document owner: HR Legal Belgium
Version: 3.2
Effective from: 1 January 2026
Effective until: 31 December 2026
Status: Active

Full-time employees working in Belgium receive 20 statutory paid leave days for the 2026 calendar year. Part-time entitlement is calculated proportionally according to the employee's contractual working schedule.

Additional company leave granted in an individual employment contract is tracked separately and is added to the statutory entitlement. Approved leave, leave already taken, and pending bookings must all be considered when calculating the number of days still available.

Employees should submit planned leave through the time-management system. A booking only becomes final after manager approval. When this policy conflicts with an older FAQ or informal HR message, this active policy takes precedence unless an employee contract provides an additional individual benefit.

Questions that cannot be answered from the current policy, employee contract, and live leave balance should be escalated to HR Operations.

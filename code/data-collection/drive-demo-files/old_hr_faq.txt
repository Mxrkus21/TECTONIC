How many paid leave days does a full-time employee in Belgium receive?

Under the policy used for this archived FAQ, a full-time Belgian employee receives 18 paid leave days per calendar year. Extra contractual leave may apply in exceptional cases and employees should contact HR for confirmation.

How can leave be requested?

Employees request leave through the time-management portal. Requests remain provisional until approved by the employee's manager.
